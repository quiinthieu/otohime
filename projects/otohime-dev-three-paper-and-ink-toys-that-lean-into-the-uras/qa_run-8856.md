It failed once on the same build that passed two earlier runs. An independent CDP pan in a fresh local production browser found `touch-action: pan-y`, scrollY 2064 → 2184 on upward pan and 2184 → 2073 on downward pan, while lazy sibling loading changed max scroll from 3904 to 4089. This proves native scrolling can work, but the delivered fixture is nondeterministic and its failed assertion cannot be attributed to a product regression. Owner: SWE for `t-1590`. Failure condition: the contract touch control can fail under an otherwise valid scroll state. Fix it by establishing room in the requested direction, stabilizing sibling layout, asserting a direction appropriate to the bounds and recording the bounds on failure; rerun all contracts and the full browser suite.
