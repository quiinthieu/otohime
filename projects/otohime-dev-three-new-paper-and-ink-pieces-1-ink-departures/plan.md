## 1. Product Outcome

Deliver three paper-and-ink interactive pieces on `otohime.dev` with zero new npm dependencies, zero database migrations, full keyboard and screen-reader accessibility, muted-by-default shared Web Audio cues, and strict `prefers-reduced-motion` support:

1. **Senko Hanabi (`/play/senko` — `app/(site)/(app)/play/senko/`, `lib/senko.ts`, `lib/sound-client.ts`)**:
   - **Press-and-Hold Japanese Sparkler Toy**: Visitors press and hold on the paper stage (via pointer/touch, keyboard `Space`/`Enter` on the hold button, or an accessible `"Steady hold"` latch) to light a sumi-ink sparkler. A glowing vermilion-and-sumi bead forms and progresses through the four classic stages by held duration:
     - **Botan** (*peony*): a glowing ember bead throwing thick, slow warm ink sparks.
     - **Matsuba** (*pine needles*): brisk, dense branching sparks that split mid-flight into 2–3 short-lived needle children.
     - **Yanagi** (*willow*): long, weeping, gravity-curved ink arcs trailing down paper.
     - **Chiri-giku** (*falling chrysanthemum*): sparse, faint dying sparks before the bead fades to `out`.
   - **Steadiness Physics**: Moving the pointer too fast while lit accumulates frame displacement jitter over a rolling window (with a more forgiving threshold for `pointerType === "touch"` than `"mouse"`/`"pen"`). Exceeding the steadiness budget causes the glowing bead to detach, drop under gravity (`dropped`), and extinguish early. Keyboard hold (`Space`/`Enter`) and the `"Steady hold"` latch satisfy steadiness trivially. Includes deterministic stage-select/advance controls (`data-testid="senko-stage-select"`, `data-testid="senko-shake-btn"`) so visitors, keyboard users, and automated probes can inspect every stage and the bead-drop failure mode immediately without waiting on wall-clock timers.
   - **Canvas & Web Audio Lifecycle**: Single `requestAnimationFrame` loop on a Canvas 2D island (`DPR <= 2`, `ResizeObserver`, ~10KB gzipped behind a `next/dynamic(..., { ssr: false })` loader, pool capped at 200 particles reusing `lib/ink-particles.ts` `seeded`). Stops `requestAnimationFrame` immediately on `document.hidden`, `blur`, `pointercancel`, `idle`/`out`/`dropped`, and unmount. Plays a quiet filtered-tick crackle cue via `lib/sound-client.ts`'s shared `AudioContext` (muted by default, gated on user gesture and `getSound()`). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, renders a calmer sparkler (fewer sparks, zero tremor/shake) while keeping all four stages playable and announced in `role="status"` (`data-testid="senko-status"`).

2. **Ink Departures Board (`components/departures-board.tsx`, `components/departures-board.css`, `lib/departures.ts`, `/work`, `/projects`)**:
   - **Split-Flap (Solari) Board in Ink & Paper**: Mounted on both `/work` (data pipeline schedules and stack runs) and `/projects` (shipped and in-flight independent projects), built deterministically from `content/site.ts` (`site.stack`, `site.pipeline`, `site.projectList`) with zero network fetches. Each row displays `{ code, name, schedule, status, note }` where `status` is deterministically `ON TIME`, `DELAYED`, or `BACKFILLING` (with hanko vermilion styling for `DELAYED` alongside explicit text, never colour-only).
   - **Scroll-Driven CSS & JS Flip Fallback**:
     - Server-renders a semantic `<table>` with the true final text in the DOM (`name`, `schedule`, `status`) so crawlers, screen readers, and no-JS environments read complete content immediately; decorative split-flap character cells (`A–Z`, `0–9`, space, punctuation) are `aria-hidden="true"`.
     - Inside `@supports (animation-timeline: view())` and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, flap leaves flip as rows enter the viewport (`view-timeline: --departure-row block; animation-timeline: --departure-row; animation-range: entry 5% entry 65%`).
     - Where `animation-timeline: view()` is unsupported — or whenever a visitor clicks a row or activates `"Flip row"` (`data-testid="departures-row-flip"`) / `"Flip board"` (`data-testid="departures-flip-all"`) — runs a bounded JS character-drum flip of at most 12 steps per cell along `flipPath(fromChar, toChar)`.
     - Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, disables both CSS scroll-timeline animations and JS multi-step flipping (`0` intermediate steps; final text shown immediately) and suppresses clack audio.
   - **Soft Clack Audio**: Includes an accessible sound toggle (`aria-pressed`, synced with `lib/sound-client.ts`, muted by default) and plays a quiet mechanical paper-flap clack cue via `lib/sound-client.ts` on user-triggered flips, throttled to at most one clack per `60 ms`.

3. **Ennichi Night (`/play/ennichi` — `app/(site)/(app)/play/ennichi/`, `lib/ennichi.ts`)**:
   - **Four-Colour "Showa Offset" Shrine-Festival Scene**: An inline SVG seek-and-find scene (`viewBox="0 0 1200 800"`, zero raster images, budget ~60–100KB) printed in four Showa offset ink plate groups (`data-plate="cyan"`, `"magenta"`, `"yellow"`, `"key"`) with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, subtle plate misregistration offsets (`--plate-dx`, `--plate-dy`, static under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), and an SVG `<pattern>` halftone dot screen. Depicts festival yatai stalls (goldfish scooping, shaved ice, kitsune masks, candied apples, chochin lanterns, torii gate, shrine steps).
   - **Seven Hidden Otohime Motifs**: Woven into the scene at fixed coordinates in `lib/ennichi.ts` (`kame-turtle`, `tamatebako`, `coral-crown`, `daruma-doll`, `hanko-seal`, `pocket-console`, `urashima-bell`), each with an effective hit target `>= 44px` (`hitRadius >= 28` in scene units) and keyboard-focusable in DOM order (`role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, unfound label reveals stall region without spoiling identity until found, then announces `"Found: <name>"`).
   - **Pan/Zoom, Hint Lantern & Commemorative Stamp**:
     - Supports pointer drag pan (with drag-vs-click threshold so panning never misfires a motif), two-pointer pinch zoom, wheel zoom, `+` / `-` / `Reset view` buttons (`data-testid="ennichi-zoom-in"`, `"ennichi-zoom-out"`, `"ennichi-zoom-reset"`), and arrow-key panning (`touch-action: none`), clamped cleanly to scene bounds.
     - Clicking `"Raise hint lantern"` (`data-testid="ennichi-hint-btn"`) selects the nearest unfound motif to the viewport center and announces a warm directional/stall region clue (`data-testid="ennichi-hint-status"`, never raw coordinates) with a cooldown and visual lantern highlight ring.
     - Found motifs persist in `localStorage` under versioned key `ennichi:v1` with tolerant parsing and a `"Reset progress"` button (`data-testid="ennichi-reset-btn"`). Finding all 7 motifs unlocks a commemorative vermilion Showa festival hanko stamp overlay (`data-testid="ennichi-stamp-unlocked"`).
