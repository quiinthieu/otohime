# QA report: pm-203 / t-1163, Planisphere island: turn the wheel (run-6833)

**Verdict: fail. Cause: code.**

On a phone, a finger cannot turn the disc. The browser treats the touch drag
as a page pan: it fires `pointercancel` after about 8 moves, and the page
scrolls. A quarter-turn drag gives **1 hour, not 6**, at 390 px. The disc's
`touch-action: none` (`app/(planisphere)/planisphere.css:153`) is set on an
SVG `<g>`, where Chromium does not apply it. Vinyl's README already notes
this ("`touch-action` does not apply inside an SVG"), and
`app/(vinyl)/_components/turntable.tsx:17-19, 81-85` works around it.

Everything else in the acceptance passes, on production at the delivery sha:

- mouse drag
- arrow keys, Shift ±1 h and crossing midnight
- Now
- Use my location, granted and denied
- reduced motion
- the URL, the live region and the form following each change
- the client chunks
- the no-JS page, pixel-identical to task 2's

The fix is in [fix-plan-t-1163.md](fix-plan-t-1163.md). It also covers one
secondary accessibility finding, the axe `nested-interactive` rule.

## Notes

- **Verdict:** I took the touch drag as the failing item. The acceptance says "dragging the disc … turns the sky", and the plan's QA asks for a quarter-turn drag "at the same viewports", 390 and 320 px being phones. A visitor on a phone gets a page scroll and about an hour of turn. The touch was synthesised through Chrome's CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, which runs Chrome's real gesture and touch-action path. The controls prove this: the same probe passes once `touch-action` is effective.
- **First-probe misses:** two were the probe's own fault and were re-proven. A drag started on `.frame-face`, the paper frame covering the disc outside the window, did nothing, as on a paper planisphere. From any point that hit-tests on the disc, the drag is exact. The first sample 60 ms after a key also missed the transition; the per-frame sampling above shows it.
- No application source was changed in this run.
