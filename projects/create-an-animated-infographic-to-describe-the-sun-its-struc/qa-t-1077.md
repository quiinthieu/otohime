## Notes (not fails)

- **Play is slower than nominal.** With motion, each chapter holds for about
  9.4 s, against a nominal 8.8 s (106 ticks at 12 fps). Under reduced motion it
  holds for about 11 s, against a nominal 8 s (96 ticks). The order and the
  stop rule are right. The timing belongs to the tour and client from t-1076,
  which this task lists as a non-goal, and `tour.test.ts` pins the rules. If it
  matters later, look at how `sun-app.tsx`'s interval reacts to the `settle`
  dispatched after each reduced-motion tick.
- **The life scene is not to scale.** The red giant is about 2.5× today's Sun,
  and the white dwarf is drawn far larger than Earth-sized. The ticks are not
  spaced by time. The scene claims no scale, so this contradicts no fact.
- **Labels are small at 390 px.** The stage scales the 800-wide SVG to about
  350 px, so its labels render at about 7 px. They are readable on a phone, and
  the same text is in the facts list and the chapter sections.
- **Wind lines touch two labels.** In the corona scene, the wind lines brush
  the "Earth and its magnetic bubble" label. This is cosmetic.
