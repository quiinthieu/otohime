## 1. Product Outcome

Deliver two paper-and-ink pocket-console experiments on `otohime.dev` with zero new npm dependencies, light bundle footprint, full keyboard and screen-reader accessibility, and strict `prefers-reduced-motion` support:

1. **Ink Blot Test (`/play/inkblot` — `app/(site)/(app)/play/inkblot/`, `lib/inkblot.ts`, `lib/inkblot-http.ts`, `lib/inkblot-store.ts`, `app/api/inkblot/answers/route.ts`)**:
   - **Fold-the-Paper Toy**: Visitors drip sumi ink on the left half of a washi sheet via pointer/touch or keyboard (arrow keys move a visible pen reticle, `Space`/`Enter` drips, plus a `"Random drops"` button), or load a deterministic blot from a shareable base36 URL seed (`?b=<seed>`) or the UTC **Daily Blot** (`daily:<YYYY-MM-DD>`, seeded from `"inkblot:" + utcDate`).
   - **Symmetric Mirror & Ink-Bleed Filter**: Clicking `"Fold sheet"` flips and mirrors the half across the central paper crease into a symmetric Rorschach blot rendered with an SVG/canvas ink-bleed filter (`feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` + `feGaussianBlur` + `feColorMatrix` threshold on a bounded `<= 640px` stage). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the 300ms CSS flip transition is skipped (`data-motion="reduced"`) for an instant swap. The blot exposes `role="img"` with deterministic descriptive `aria-label` text (lobe count, spread, density, splatter count, seed).
   - **Patients' Notes Gallery & Playful Pseudo-Reading**: Visitors type what they see (`1..80` code points, optional name `0..24` code points). Submitting computes an immediate, deterministic **Pseudo-Reading** card (slotted from 40+ templates and 20+ fragments using blot geometry and the visitor's words, clearly labelled `"A paper-and-ink toy — not medical or psychological advice."`) and stores the observation via `POST /api/inkblot/answers` into an expand-only `inkblot_answer` + `inkblot_usage` Postgres schema reusing the Guestbook moderation/quota pipeline (NFKC normalization, profanity/URL rejection, honeypot, Origin check, HMAC IP/hour limit of 3, daily limit of 100, pending cap of 500, cursor pagination of approved notes per `blot_key`). The visitor's submitted note is pinned immediately in their handwritten **"Patients' Notes"** gallery alongside approved public notes from `GET /api/inkblot/answers?blot=<blotKey>` and deterministic curated ward specimen notes.
   - **OG Card & `/play` Discovery**: Includes `app/(site)/(app)/play/inkblot/opengraph-image.tsx` (plain SVG symmetric blot for satori) and a discovery card (`data-testid="play-inkblot-link"`) on `/play`.

2. **Shimi Bookworms (`shimi/`, `lib/shimi.ts`, `components/kame/loader.tsx`, `components/handheld.tsx`, `components/pocket-terminal.tsx`, `lib/terminal.ts`, `lib/sound-client.ts`)**:
   - **Generative Ink Silverfish Swarm**: A swarm of 3–5 seeded ink silverfish (*shimi*, the paper-eating bugs of old Japanese books) with 7–9 tapered segments, sine-wave legs, twin antennae, and three caudal cerci rendered on a fixed, full-viewport `aria-hidden="true"`, `pointer-events: none` `<canvas data-shimi-canvas="">` (capped at `<= 30 fps`, DPR `<= 2`, paused on `document.hidden`, faint ink trails with alpha `<= 0.15` so text contrast stays WCAG AA).
   - **Reading Real DOM Text & Overlay-Only Nibbling**: Walks readable text nodes inside `main` via `TreeWalker` on a throttled Range rect cache (skipping `script`, `style`, `input`, `textarea`, `select`, `code`, `pre`, `button`, `nav`, `form`, `dialog`, `[contenteditable]`, `[aria-hidden="true"]`, and `[data-shimi-ignore]`, with zero layout reads inside `requestAnimationFrame`). Nibbling is **100% overlay-only**: creatures draw paper-colored notches over characters on the canvas (capped at 12 active notches) and never mutate DOM text nodes. Hovering near a nibbled word (`pointermove`) or focusing inside its element smoothly restores the notch.
   - **Pentatonic Web Audio Chirps**: Nibbling a character computes a pentatonic frequency (`260..880 Hz`) from `charCodeAt` and plays a `30..80 ms` sine/triangle chirp (`<= -26 dB`, max 3 voices, max 8 chirps/sec) through `lib/sound-client.ts`'s single shared `AudioContext`, muted unless the site `SoundToggle` is on and suspended on blur/pagehide.
   - **Markov Diet & Notes Speech Bubbles**: Crawled words feed a bounded local order-1 Markov chain (`<= 2,000` tokens, stripping URLs and emails) that remixes 4–10 word `'burp'` phrases in a 3s speech bubble (`data-shimi-bubble=""`, `aria-hidden="true"`). Automatic burps occur on `/notes` and `/notes/[slug]` while summoned, and an accessible control pill (`data-shimi-pill=""`) provides both a `"Burp phrase"` button (`data-shimi-burp-trigger=""`) and an on/off toggle (`aria-pressed="true"`, dismissible with `Escape`).
   - **Off by Default, Summon Points & Reduced Motion**: Off by default on all pages (`localStorage.getItem("shimi") === "on"`). Summoned or dismissed from: (a) the Pocket console chip (`data-pocket-shimi=""`), (b) the Pocket Terminal command `shimi [on|off|toggle]`, or (c) typing `"shimi"` outside form fields and open dialogs. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, never auto-starts; when explicitly summoned, renders a static resting pose (`data-shimi-mode="static"`, zero ongoing `requestAnimationFrame` loop) and emits burp phrases only on button press.
