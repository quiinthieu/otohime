### The checks that failed

- **"pinch-zoom allowed on scroller"** (every run) was a false negative in my check. Chrome reports the computed
  `touch-action: pan-x pan-y pinch-zoom` as `manipulation`, which is the same value under its alias.
  The CSS in `app/(elements)/elements.css` (`.scroller`) is correct.
- **Production run 1 only:** "Space opens Iron" missed once, 100 ms after a Close click, and the backdrop check
  that follows it failed as a knock-on. I could not reproduce it:
  - a focused probe opened Iron with Space 9/9 times, both fresh and after Enter+Close
  - two more full production runs passed it
  - the local run passed it

  I treat it as a timing flake in my script, which samples 100 ms after the key press against a remote host. It is not a defect: the keyup-to-open path in
  `table.tsx` is sound. This is recorded as a decision.
